SectorPad overlay directions

Three Starsector-inspired options for the pointer, keyboard, navigation selection, and aiming reticle. Choose a direction to inspect it at the 1280 × 800 reference size.

Design history · 7 September 2026
TriPad selected and implemented

The sheets compare visual treatments using the same layout. They are a composite study, not a screenshot of four simultaneous game modes.

What stays consistent

  • The native arrow remains visible at its true hotspot. Every pointer mark is a new, additive companion.
  • Selection has a border and a shape cue. Color alone never identifies focus, precision, or lock.
  • All aiming marks have a clear centre and a dark under-stroke. The lock marker reports an actual lock.
  • The keyboard keeps the current letters, punctuation, and footer actions. Text stays staged until accepted.

What to choose

  • TriPad gives the closest match to the installed cyan and amber menus.
  • Survey is the restrained option; its open arcs are the most delicate of the three.
  • Shipboard emphasizes contrast and selection with stronger shapes and larger lettering.
  • Size, opacity, and reduced motion should be independent settings, so readability does not require switching the whole theme.
