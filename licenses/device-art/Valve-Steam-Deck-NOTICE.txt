Valve supplies these front, top and back SVG views specifically for adding
Steam Deck input callouts in games. SectorPad uses the front illustration for
its in-game controller mapping and live input display under that permission.
This is not a CC0/MIT relicensing or an assertion of Valve endorsement.

Adaptations: front SVG rendered to an antialiased transparent PNG with white
ink for runtime tinting. Mod code draws live-input and mapping highlights as
separate overlays. Original SVG geometry is retained.
